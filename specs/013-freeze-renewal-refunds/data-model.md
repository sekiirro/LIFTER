# F13 Logical Data Model

These are logical entities and invariants, not SQL definitions or authorization grants. F13 extends F12 forward-only.

| Entity                                 | Ownership and key relationships                                                                                                        | Invariants                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Continuous subscription and paid terms | One F12 tenant-scoped subscription identity per client; immutable original and renewal purchase snapshots                              | Upgrade/renewal revise current entitlement on the same subscription; no successor subscription row; payment facts and issued obligations remain immutable                                                                                                   |
| Immutable purchase record              | Tenant-scoped original-package purchase, upgrade charge, or renewal purchase linked to the continuous subscription and its paid period | Retains purchase/period identity, collections and refundable balance; original package plus its upgrade charges share a refundable package-group identity, while each renewal remains a separate group; tenant composite keys prevent cross-workspace joins |
| Activation and entitlement interval    | F12 activation seam plus F13-derived Cairo service interval                                                                            | No initial service interval before P4 activation; current entitlement derives from the continuous-subscription term and counted freeze contributions                                                                                                        |
| Freeze facts                           | Workspace/client/subscription with immutable Apply, optional Stop/Remove and lifecycle revision                                        | Duration 1–30 calendar days; no overlap, sequential allowed; Apply date included, Stop date excluded; Remove contribution zero without rewriting historical access                                                                                          |
| Installment obligation                 | Existing F12 immutable issued obligation                                                                                               | Renewal due-date anchor is Cairo recording date; freeze/service shifts do not alter due dates; cancellation uses separate unique fact                                                                                                                       |
| Collection/correction stream           | Existing F12 payment event chain                                                                                                       | Preserve exact full correction reversal, one-reversal uniqueness, recollection semantics and original rows                                                                                                                                                  |
| Refund operation                       | Aggregate receipt identity, actor/key, selected refundable package group, amount, expected revisions and refund date                   | PO-only, explicit external confirmation, positive amount, one selected package group, no gateway, idempotent binding; allocations may span the original package and its upgrade charges but never a separate renewal group                                  |
| Refund allocation event                | Tenant-scoped immutable child of refund operation and source collection                                                                | Negative principal and commission; multiple operations may allocate against one source; FIFO; source and purchase cumulative caps; never uses correction reversal key                                                                                       |
| Obligation cancellation                | Immutable fact linked to one unpaid obligation and refund operation                                                                    | Unique per obligation; canceled obligations cannot be collected/corrected/reopened                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Client deactivation/Auth state         | Immutable refund-linked lifecycle fact and fail-closed login/session state                                                             | One deactivation; the Custom Access Token hook denies all new issuance after commit, and one Auth Admin outbox job applies a retryable defense-in-depth ban; later refunds do not reactivate or duplicate lockout                                           |
| Operation receipt and audit            | Existing private idempotency/audit mechanisms extended                                                                                 | Authorization before disclosure; exact replay is effect-free; safe fields only; failure rolls back all writes                                                                                                                                               |

All tenant-owned entities require `workspace_id`, composite tenant-preserving FKs, forced default-deny RLS and narrowly granted SECURITY DEFINER operations. Financial rows are append-only for application roles. No table shape is finalized until merged F12 U4 is inspected.

## S43-PO-5/6 continuity model delta (2026-11-10)

PO source wording: S43-PO-5 and S43-PO-6 lines are preserved byte-exact in spec.md under Clarifications / PO-decision register. Architect interpretation is separately labelled **architect mapping (S43-R6/R7)**.

**architect mapping (S43-R7/R8/R9/R10):** Preserve the single subscription row and immutable original/renewal payment facts. Same-rank longer and higher-rank equal/longer upgrades use the original start; after an expired renewal, the renewal start is the anchor; higher-rank shorter upgrades use the upgrade date; pre-activation duration starts at activation. Renewal-then-upgrade credits only the original package payment and preserves separate renewal duration and payment. Full refunds cover selected package payment including upgrade charges; separate renewals are not automatically refunded. No prorated upgrade credit; new revenue is additional payments only. Initial activation is written once; renewal/upgrade append lifecycle/ledger events without new activation rows. S43-PO-6 resolves F13 MCQ-7..11; S43-PO-8 resolves the tier structure, post-lapse renewal-start anchor, and same-currency-only rule.

### Logical state and integrity requirements

- One subscription identity per client; renewal and upgrade append immutable operation/payment/audit facts and revise entitlement under expected-revision concurrency. No successor subscription rows.
- Preserve payment facts so credit, refund allocation, and renewal-duration preservation can be reconciled without overwriting financial history. Credit only the original package payment in renewal-then-upgrade; separate renewal payments are not automatically refunded.
- Keep immutable purchase records for the original package, each upgrade charge, and each renewal, linked to the one subscription and the paid period they fund. Assign the original package and its upgrade charges to one refundable package group; each separately purchased renewal has its own group. Collection totals, upgrade credit and refundable balances must remain attributable to their source purchases; a full-package refund may allocate across purchases in the selected group, but never across groups. Tenant composite keys are required. Do not consume a separate renewal's payment when refunding the upgraded original package.
- Represent original/current start and expiry so same-tier longer uses original start, higher-tier shorter uses operation date, active renewal adds to current expiry, expired renewal anchors to today, and counted freeze extends expiry. Higher-tier equal/longer uses original start; pre-activation duration starts at activation.
- Guards reject upgrade/renewal while frozen, reject upgrade after expiry, permit pre-activation upgrade, and permit expired renewal from today. Equal-price upgrade has no charge/revenue event; lower-price upgrade has no mutation.
- Productivity uniqueness is per new-client identity, independent of lapse, renewals, upgrades, or plan publications.
- Refund references the full selected-package group, including its original package and upgrade charges; separate renewal groups and payments are not automatically refunded. A full-package refund may allocate across the group's source purchases without violating the one-group selection rule; separate renewals require their own explicit selection. Validate with refund-after-upgrade and renewal-then-upgrade scenarios that leave renewal collections and refundable balances untouched.

### S43-PO-6 / S43-R8 lifecycle representation

The PO wording and architect interpretation are separated in spec.md. **architect mapping (S43-R8):** Activation is written once. Renewal and upgrade append their own lifecycle/ledger facts and do not write a new activation row. New writes do not use activation_source = 'renewal'; historic dev rows are disposable mock data. Renewal still restores/extends access when recorded.
