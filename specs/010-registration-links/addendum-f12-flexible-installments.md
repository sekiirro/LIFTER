## Final anchor and historical preservation

- Installment 1 is the upfront amount collected at approval. A legacy stored first offset is retained unchanged; it is not used to postpone upfront collection until activation.
- For installment n>=2, `due_on = Africa/Cairo calendar date of activation + issued due_offset_days`, payable through that Cairo day-end (before the following Cairo midnight). Until activation exists, due date is undefined and later collection is denied.
- Default split timing is upfront at approval and one remainder at offset 30 Cairo calendar days after activation. Upfront amount is chosen explicitly; no default amount/percentage is approved. Full-price upfront is one payment with no zero remainder.
- Custom count, amounts and offsets remain supported. Every issued agreement is preserved, including second offsets other than 30; there is no normalization, reissue, hold, historical UPDATE or default backfill.
- Positive amounts, contiguous sequences, existing increasing nonnegative integer offsets, tenant integrity and exact total remain enforced. Early payment is allowed after activation and only in sequence. Corrections restore an obligation to unpaid without editing any financial event.

## Implementation ownership and limits

F12 U1F/T049–T054 owns future `20261101131000_flexible_registration_link_terms_forward.sql`, existing issuing action/service/validation, dialog/manager/CSS/messages and targeted RED→GREEN tests. New wrapper receives explicit upfront/count and full schedule, verifies correspondence, then delegates to existing atomic guarded issuance. It does not add a parallel schedule table or rewrite F10 migrations. Existing legacy RPC/null-schedule behavior remains valid.

Tests must compare fingerprints of already-issued link/schedule rows before/after the extension, cover custom first/second offsets, default residual/30 constructor, exact-total/count/upfront tampering, role denials, deferred rollback, F11 signup consumption and no public financial/token/commission leakage. Q-M19..Q-M24 and affected earlier checks are owned by the F12 verification plan.

The [Frontend Design Gate](../../docs/09-frontend-design-gate.md) and [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) apply. Count control/removal interaction, exact copy and focus/badge details are architect review items; missing DS decisions are escalated through the gate, not invented by implementation. F10's unrelated issuance-limit, currency/catalog and production-readiness items are not decided by this addendum.
