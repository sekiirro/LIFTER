# Feature Specification: F19 Shared Plan Core

**Feature Branch**: `speckit-019-plan-core`
**Phase**: Specify → Clarify → Plan → Tasks → Analyze (documentation only)
**Status**: Spec-only proposal encoding approved PO decisions; implementation is not authorized by this change.

## Purpose and scope

F19 defines the shared lifecycle, delivery, activation, and client projection contract used by F17 Workout Planner and F18 Nutrition Planner. F19 owns stable plan identity, private drafts, immutable published revisions, delivery records, initial-subscription activation, productivity-unit attribution, authorization boundaries, and the shared client/delivery contract. F17 and F18 own their respective content schemas. F16 owns catalog identities, version references, and live exercise-video resolution. F12/F13 own subscription and renewal facts. P7 consumes productivity-unit records.

Workout-only and nutrition-only subscription types are out of scope for launch (A1). Real-time co-editing is out of scope (A3). This unit writes no implementation, migration, application code, or test code.

## User scenarios

1. **Edit and publish:** Staff edits one stable plan. Autosave and Save as Draft update its private draft. Ready & Publish appends an immutable revision; it does not create another Plan ID.
2. **Create or copy:** Only explicit Create New Plan creates a new root. It may start blank, from a template, or from an authorized existing plan. Copying reproduces planner-entry/content values, not ownership or history.
3. **Deliver and activate:** Ready & Publish for a client with a current subscription is the delivery operation. Workout and nutrition deliveries may occur on different days. The initial subscription activates once both kinds have a committed delivery; that same transaction writes exactly one productivity unit. Later deliveries and revisions cannot reactivate or add units.
4. **Override:** A Platform Owner may Override Activate an eligible initial subscription before, between, or after plan deliveries. The operation immediately activates and writes one unit, without creating a delivery. Later plan deliveries do not add another unit.
5. **Client access:** The client projection contains every delivered plan for that client and exposes the latest published revision of each. Undelivered plans and private drafts are never disclosed. Expiry blocks normal platform content and presents the approved renewal page body.

## Functional requirements

- **F19-FR-001 - Stable plan lifecycle (baseline B).** A plan has one stable Plan ID, one private mutable autosaved draft, and append-only immutable published revisions. Draft saves and publish retries do not create roots. Only explicit Create New Plan creates a root. Save as Draft and Ready & Publish are distinct actions; draft content is never client-visible. Every Ready & Publish appends one revision and records one delivery for its target subscription. Later revisions remain under the same Plan ID and do not create another root or reactivate the subscription.
- **F19-FR-002 - Plan ownership, subscription selection, and delivery (A5, Q1, MCQ-12/16).** Each plan root carries `workspace_id` and `client_id` and is bound to a subscription by its delivery rows. Ready & Publish is one action: each successful published revision atomically appends that revision and one `plan_deliveries` row for the selected subscription; there is no separate Deliver action. Initial subscriptions in F13 `awaiting_activation` are eligible F19 delivery targets so the approved two-delivery activation can occur. Otherwise select the subscription whose F13 entitlement projection is `active` or `frozen` (F13 FR-028; `app_private.f13_project_entitlement` in `20261103130000_f13_review_guards_projection.sql`); do not select a `future`, `expired`, `refunded`, or cancelled subscription. An early renewal remains `future` until its service start, so its predecessor remains current through the handoff. When no eligible subscription exists, Ready & Publish is refused and writes no revision or delivery. If more than one eligible subscription exists, selection is PO-DECISION-NEEDED MCQ-16.
- **F19-FR-003 - All delivered plans visible (A5).** A client may have any number of distinct plans. The authorized client projection returns every plan with a committed `plan_deliveries` row for that client, including multiple plans of the same kind. It must not omit plans because they lack a special "first delivery" record. Drafts without a delivery are not disclosed. Because publishing without an eligible subscription is refused, no published revision can be undelivered.
- **F19-FR-004 — Initial automatic activation (A1, Q2/Q3).** For an `initial` subscription, the first committed workout delivery and first committed nutrition delivery for that same workspace, client, and subscription satisfy the activation condition, even when they occur on different days. The delivery completing the pair, activation, and one productivity-unit row commit in one atomic transaction. The first delivery remains committed if the other kind is delivered later. The activation is unique per `(workspace_id, subscription_id)`. Later deliveries or revisions never reactivate or add units. Workout-only and nutrition-only subscription types are deferred and excluded.
- **F19-FR-005 — Renewal activation (A1, Q2=A, Q3=A).** F13’s S22-PO-5 renewal behavior remains authoritative: a renewal activation is recorded atomically by `record_renewal` at recording, requires no plan delivery, and earns no productivity unit. F19 does not create a second renewal activation path.
- **F19-FR-006 — Platform Owner override (A2, Q4=A, Q6=B, Q9).** Only a Platform Owner may invoke Override Activate, and only for an initial subscription. It is allowed for any otherwise eligible initial subscription, including an unapproved or unpaid one. It is refused for cancelled or refunded subscriptions and for deactivated clients, returning the existing generic `not_permitted` result. Override immediately records activation and exactly one productivity unit in one transaction, even with zero plan deliveries. It records no fabricated plan-delivery row. Subsequent plan deliveries cannot activate again or add another unit. Override is not available for renewal subscriptions.
- **F19-FR-007 - Productivity-unit recipient (Q5=A, Q10=No, MCQ-14).** Automatic activation credits the trainer assigned at the activation transaction serialization point. If no trainer is assigned, credit the Trainer who published the completing delivery; if that publisher is not a Trainer, refuse the completing Ready & Publish with no delivery, activation, or unit. Override with no assignment requires selection of an active trainer in that workspace. Selection records only the unit recipient and never creates or changes an assignment. One append-only unit is written per activation; F19 owns it and P7 consumes it. Renewals create no unit.
- **F19-FR-008 — Copy semantics (A4, Q7=A).** Copying an existing plan uses its latest published revision; copying a template uses its immutable template version. Copy actual planner-entry values, including sets, reps, RIR, rest, Notes, and nutrition content, rather than current library defaults. Create independent destination values. Do not copy source ownership, authorship, audit history, client/subscription association, delivery or activation facts. Do not copy exercise Video: F16 resolves Video live for the authorized workspace. F17/F18 define domain-specific content fields.
- **F19-FR-009 — Editing permissions (A3, Q8=A).** A Trainer, Workspace Owner, or Platform Owner with current authorization in the plan’s workspace may edit any client plan in that workspace, including one associated with another trainer. Sales may not edit. No role receives cross-workspace plan access. The system provides no real-time co-editing experience.
- **F19-FR-010 — Compare-and-swap writes (A3, Q8=A).** Draft writes compare the expected draft version; publish compares the expected current draft and published revision/version. A stale write returns `stale_version` without partial mutation. Exact idempotency-key replay returns the original committed result after current authorization is rechecked; changed arguments under the same key return `key_conflict`. Concurrent operations cannot overwrite a newer draft or append duplicate revisions/deliveries.
- **F19-FR-011 — Expired client access (A6, Q11).** On subscription expiry, normal platform content, including plan content, is blocked and the client sees a renewal page encouraging contact with their trainer. The approved EN and AR body strings are copied byte-for-byte below. Page title and button strings are not approved; see PO-DECISION-NEEDED MCQ-11. F19 defines the plan-content denial boundary and projection; P4 owns the wider access enforcement and expiry surface.
- **F19-FR-012 - Trainer deletion and retained history (A7, MCQ-13).** Trainer deletion must not cascade-delete plans, revisions, deliveries, activations, productivity records, or audit history. Author references use `ON DELETE RESTRICT` or equivalent and retain the deleted trainer non-login account ID under F11 deletion policy; no tombstone rewrite is used. F11 PR #73 remains pending, not a merged fact.
- **F19-FR-013 — Audit, tenant isolation, and persistence.** All plan, revision, delivery, activation, and unit operations are workspace-scoped, authorization-checked after relevant lock waits, idempotent, auditable, and concurrency-safe. Client self-reads derive the client from authenticated identity and never accept a caller-selected client/workspace as authority. Persistence and concurrency evidence requires real-database integration tests; static or mocked tests do not establish persistence.
- **F19-FR-014 — Forward-only activation model.** `subscription_activations` is extended only by a forward migration design. It has `activation_source` in `plan_deliveries`, `po_override`, `renewal`. Typed workout/nutrition delivery links, when present, reference `plan_deliveries` rows only and are constrained to the corresponding kind and same workspace/client/subscription. Source-specific checks require exactly the valid link shape: both typed links for `plan_deliveries`; neither for `po_override` or `renewal`. `delivery_event_id` remains non-null and is the source operation ID (the renewal operation ID for renewal activations). Never fabricate delivery rows. Unclassifiable legacy activation rows block migration pending evidence; do not guess or rewrite historical meaning.

## Measurable success criteria and acceptance scenarios

- **SC-001 — Stable private lifecycle (baseline B).** For every successful draft save, the Plan ID remains unchanged; zero client projections contain the draft; each successful publish appends one immutable revision. **Given** Plan P with revision R, **When** authorized staff edit and save, **Then** P remains P and the draft is private. **When** Ready & Publish succeeds, **Then** one new immutable revision is appended under P and the draft becomes the published content.
- **SC-002 — Explicit creation only (baseline B).** Across 100 retries of autosave, Save as Draft, and Ready & Publish with the same operation key, exactly zero additional roots are created; an explicit Create New Plan creates exactly one root. **Given** an existing plan, **When** any edit/save/publish operation is retried, **Then** its root count is unchanged. **When** Create New Plan succeeds, **Then** exactly one new Plan ID exists.
- **SC-003 - Delivery is Ready & Publish (Q1=A, MCQ-12/16).** Each successful Ready & Publish for an eligible subscription publishes exactly one immutable revision and records exactly one delivery row for that revision/subscription. **Given** an authorized editor and one eligible subscription S, **When** Ready & Publish commits, **Then** revision and delivery for S commit together. **Given** no eligible subscription, **When** Ready & Publish is attempted, **Then** it is refused and neither revision nor delivery is created. **Given** multiple eligible subscriptions, **When** Ready & Publish is attempted, **Then** behavior remains blocked by MCQ-16.
- **SC-004 — First deliveries activate initial subscription once (A1).** In all tested orderings, a single first-kind delivery leaves activation absent; after both workout and nutrition deliveries for the same initial subscription commit, exactly one activation and one unit exist. **Given** initial subscription S and no deliveries, **When** workout is delivered, **Then** S is not activated. **When** nutrition is delivered on the same or a later day, **Then** S, one activation, and one unit commit atomically. Reversing the kinds yields the same result.
- **SC-005 — Retry, concurrency, and rollback (baseline B).** In 100 concurrent/replayed delivery attempts for one request and subscription, the database contains no duplicate delivery for one operation, revision, activation, or unit. A failure before transaction commit leaves none of that transaction’s writes. **Given** two concurrent second-kind deliveries or an unknown client result after commit, **When** callers retry the same operation key, **Then** one original receipt and at most one activation/unit are returned.
- **SC-006 — Later plans/revisions do not reactivate (A5).** For any number of later plan deliveries and revisions on an activated initial subscription, activation count and unit count remain one. **Given** an activated subscription, **When** a tenth distinct plan or a later revision is delivered, **Then** it is visible to the client and neither activation nor unit count increases.
- **SC-007 - All and only delivered plans project (A5, MCQ-12).** For a fixture with at least ten delivered plans of the same kind and one undelivered draft, every delivered plan appears and the draft is absent. **Given** delivered plans P1...P10 and draft P11, **When** the authorized client reads plans, **Then** P1...P10 appear with their latest published revisions and P11 and all draft data are absent.
- **SC-008 - Override exactly once (A2, Q4=A, Q6=B, Q9, MCQ-15).** For an otherwise eligible initial subscription with zero or one delivered plan kind, one authorized Override creates one activation and one unit immediately; a later delivery does not add another. Cancelled/refunded subscriptions and deactivated clients return `not_permitted` and create no rows. **Given** an eligible initial subscription with no deliveries, **When** a Platform Owner overrides, **Then** one activation and one unit commit with no delivery row. **Given** a subscription already activated automatically after both delivery kinds, **When** Override is attempted, **Then** no second activation or unit is created; its result remains PO-DECISION-NEEDED MCQ-15.
- **SC-009 - Recipient and assignment separation (Q5=A, Q10=No, MCQ-14).** Automatic activation credits the assigned trainer or, if none exists, the completing delivery publisher when that actor is a Trainer. Otherwise the completing Ready & Publish is refused with no writes. Override with no assignment requires an active same-workspace trainer selection. Selection never creates an assignment. **Given** an unassigned client and Trainer publisher completing delivery, **When** activation completes, **Then** that Trainer receives the unit. **Given** a non-Trainer publisher, **When** completing delivery is attempted, **Then** it is refused without delivery, activation, or unit. **Given** Override with no assigned trainer, **When** a valid trainer is selected, **Then** the unit names that trainer and assignment is unchanged.
- **SC-010 — Copy and role boundaries (A3/A4, Q7/Q8).** Copied values equal the source’s latest published revision or immutable template version for all domain content fields, including sets/reps/RIR/rest/Notes and nutrition content; Video, ownership, history, subscription, and delivery are not copied. Trainer, Workspace Owner, and Platform Owner can edit within workspace; Sales and cross-workspace actors cannot. **Given** a source whose planner values differ from current defaults, **When** it is copied, **Then** the destination preserves source values and has independent ownership/history and no copied Video value. **When** Sales or a foreign-workspace actor attempts edit/read, **Then** access is denied without revealing existence.
- **SC-011 — Expiry and trainer deletion (A6/A7, Q11).** After expiry, client plan content is denied and the approved body copy is byte-identical to the PO source. Trainer deletion leaves plan, revision, and audit record counts unchanged; author references restrict deletion and prevent cascade. **Given** an expired client, **When** the client requests a plan, **Then** content is denied and the approved renewal body is rendered. **Given** a trainer referenced as an author, **When** the F11 deletion workflow executes, **Then** authored plans/revisions/history remain and no cascading delete occurs; author reference retains the non-login account ID under F11 policy; PR #73 is pending.
- **SC-012 - Automatic activation recipient (Q5=A, MCQ-14).** Automatic activation credits the assigned trainer at serialization, or otherwise the Trainer who publishes the completing delivery. A non-Trainer completing publisher cannot complete the operation. **Given** an unassigned client with one delivery kind committed, **When** a Trainer publishes the other kind, **Then** one delivery, activation, and unit commit atomically for that Trainer. **Given** the completing publisher is a Workspace Owner or Platform Owner and no trainer is assigned, **When** Ready & Publish is attempted, **Then** no delivery, activation, or unit is committed.

Recommendation: A. Blocked: Ready & Publish only when multiple eligible subscriptions overlap; single-eligible cases remain specified.
