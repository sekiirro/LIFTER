## S70-PO-18 answers applied

1. Closed months never change. Late corrections/refund reversals post in current open month linked to original month.
2. One monthly Basic Salary per employee/person in PO-picked allowed market currency (Egypt EGP; International EGP/USD/SAR); points remain per workspace. S70-R95 assigns ownership to platform PO.
3. Initial point period is Africa/Cairo trigger month: client activation, approval+confirmed first payment, check-in 24h deadline, or persisted plan deadline. Keep trigger and recorded times separately.
4. Copy approval is per screen batch; server/back-end work continues while UI copy awaits PO approval.

## Technical decisions

- **Durable ledger**: Keep immutable F19/F10 sources; upon qualification, append a separate minimal P7 earned fact. No client FK/PII in retained staff accounting. Apply every source once by opaque unique key.
- **Refunds**: Any completed refund reverses the acquisition Sales point once. Full-refund classification comes from F13 refundable collected funds for purchase scope at that time; questionnaire/deductions only on full refund.
- **Quality revisions**: Preserve immutable revisions; only latest effective revision for each Trainer/month/category contributes. A correction replaces projection contribution, not sums old full revisions.
- **Decimals**: Exact numeric scale 2, canonical decimal strings over APIs, no JS floating-point; quality weights capped at two decimals.
- **Concurrency**: Shared workspace+month advisory fence for every producer and close, after existing source locks in a fixed order. Closed-period events route to current open month. Unique logical keys prevent retries/different request IDs from duplicating effects.
- **Payroll**: One person-level monthly salary and one approval per employee/month entitlement. Approval binds to reviewed immutable snapshot version and amount/currency; manual money inputs are PO-controlled exact minor units and never derive from points.
- **Retention**: P7 earned/adjustment/snapshot rows preserve only minimum staff facts. F26-linked follow-up/questionnaire records are purgeable and use persisted dispute holds; audit_events is not KPI truth.
- **Access**: Sales cannot infer raw activity through UI/API/RPC/RLS/export/Realtime/count/HEAD/notification/cache/replay. Only own finalized total after month-end and finalization.
- **Strikes**: Existing strike source and PO correction remain separate; P7 points are not strikes.
