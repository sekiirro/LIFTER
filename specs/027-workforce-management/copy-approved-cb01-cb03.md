## Compliance notes

- **No payouts**: every payroll string frames approval as recording an external payment already made (`workforce.payroll.approve.action`, `.confirmBody`, `.success`); no provider, transfer or disbursement wording.
- **No automatic salary deductions or discipline**: `workforce.payroll.input.*` states "Manual inputs"/"manual, PO-only"; the category `deduction` is an entered accounting adjustment, never an automatic salary deduction. Quality/point strings do not say points change salary (FR-006). No strike, warning, discipline or firing wording anywhere in either batch.
- **Sales confidentiality (matrix rows 12–13)**: no KPI/points/formula/peer-total string appears in any Sales-reachable copy. CB-01 is Trainer-only and denies Sales. In CB-03, Sales reaches only `workforce.compensation.own.*` (month, basic salary, currency, own record) — no point or KPI wording. `workforce.quality.*` (including "Points per item" and the +1/-1 hint) is PO-only.
- **PII in notification**: `workforce.reminder.*` contains no name, point, amount, staff id or client detail.
