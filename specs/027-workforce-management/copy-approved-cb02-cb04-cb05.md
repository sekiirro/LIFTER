## Compliance notes

- **No salary, payout or discipline implications**: CB-02 point strings say "Points are separate from salary" and manual edits are PO-entered with a reason; refund deductions are explicitly point deductions. CB-04 contains no money wording. CB-05 strike copy uses تأخيرات الخطط, is review-only, and states corrections never change points; dispute copy uses التواصل and points-only penalties.
- **Sales confidentiality (matrix rows 6–7 / 12–13)**: CB-04 displays only a period label and the caller's own finalized total; no formula, reason, client detail, live/other-employee total, rank, peer comparison or leaderboard reuse. Pre-finalization and denied states reveal no existence or activity. CB-01/CB-03 Sales-reachable copy carries no KPI or peer-total wording.
- **Closed vs current month**: finalization is permanent; later corrections are booked in the current open month with `workforce.report.ledger.originalMonth`, never by reopening or restating the closed month and never changing a previously displayed Sales total (FR-014/FR-021).
- **Append-only and idempotent**: strike corrections, quality revisions and point penalties append; originals stay in history; reasons are required; a duplicate or stale request rejects without mutation.
- **PO only**: the CB-02 and CB-05 screens are PO-only; denied strings say only the Platform Owner (مالك المنصة) can act and disclose no existence or month detail. CB-04 is Sales-only and reaches no other employee's data.
- **Terminology**: Platform Owner = مالك المنصة; follow-up = التواصل / اتواصل (client form = المتابعة الأسبوعية); strike = تأخير الخطة / تأخيرات الخطط; points are separate from salary (النقاط منفصلة عن المرتب).
- **No approval inferred**: status identifiers (Closed, Current month, Finalized, Disputed, Effective, Earlier revision) are requirement identifiers, not approvals of any other screen's copy.
